S³ / 01

Small systems. Big ideas. Greater impact.

S³ — Smart Small Systems — is a research laboratory dedicated to the modeling, analysis and design of multiphysics systems at the micro and nano scale. Our core competence is not one device, but a way of thinking: build rigorous physical models of small systems, then make them smart with data, learning and control.

◇ Vision

To become a recognised research school in intelligent micro/nano systems, where non-classical mechanics, physics-informed AI and digital twins are combined to solve real problems in health, energy, smart agriculture and precision engineering.

◈ Mission

  • Develop size-dependent models of nanostructures (nonlocal, strain-gradient, couple-stress, surface elasticity).
  • Predict and control electromechanical instability — pull-in under electrostatic, Casimir and van der Waals forces.
  • Turn models into research assets: open code, datasets, interactive simulators and digital twins.
  • Train MSc & BSc researchers through a coherent, five-axis research platform.

“There's Plenty of Room at the Bottom”

On 29 December 1959, Richard Feynman told the American Physical Society at Caltech that nothing in physics forbids writing the whole encyclopaedia on the head of a pin, building tiny machines, or arranging atoms one by one. Our lab lives in the room he described.

TodayNEMS resonators weigh single molecules; MEMS sit in every phone — Feynman's room is now an industry
